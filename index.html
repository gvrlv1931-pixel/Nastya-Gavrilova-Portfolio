About Me

I'm Nastya, a systems entrepreneur building circular economy infrastructure. I come at it from three directions: HR and people operations, environmental science, and community organising.

I'm the founder of StuffShare and an MSc International HRM student at Strathclyde Business School, with a BSc in Environmental Geoscience from Edinburgh.

The climate crisis is the most urgent challenge we face, and it is the one that sets everything else off balance. That's where I want to contribute. My approach is systems thinking anchored in operations and management: looking at the bigger picture, asking what's missing, finding the leverage points, and building the processes and tools that remove the friction. I want to help build a world where people are a part of the planetary ecosystem, living well alongside other species in our one shared home.

Outside of that, I'm usually up a mountain, through a new culture, or deep in a conversation that shifts something. I backpack, hitchhike, host strangers on my couch, and say yes to things I don't yet know how to do. My current one is improv.
